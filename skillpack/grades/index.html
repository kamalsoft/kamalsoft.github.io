Grades

How the skills are graded

Every skill is scored 1 to 5 on five criteria. The weighted sum gives a score out of 5, and the score gives the grade.

These are design grades: no skill has yet been run against a model with measured results. The reference project is a team that builds and runs a production web application, with a front end, APIs, a database, third-party dependencies and CI.

Criteria and weights

WeightCriterionWhat it measures
35%Support strengthDrives a correct, complete, checkable result.
20%ImportanceHow much the outcome matters.
20%SeverityHow bad what it catches is.
15%UsageHow often a project needs it.
10%PresenceHow many projects have it.

Score = 0.35 S + 0.20 I + 0.20 V + 0.15 U + 0.10 P

How the score is calculated

  1. Score each criterion from 1 to 5. 5 means the criterion is fully met, 3 means partly, 1 means not met.
  2. Multiply each score by its weight. Support strength counts 35%, importance 20%, severity 20%, usage 15% and presence 10%.
  3. Add the five results. The total is the score, out of 5, to two decimal places.
  4. Find the grade. The score sets the grade band.

Worked example: rlm-security-audit

CriterionScore (1 to 5)WeightPoints

Score = 1.75 + 1.00 + 1.00 + 0.45 + 0.504.70 → grade A+

Usage is 3 because a full security audit runs per release, not per change. The other criteria are at their highest.
